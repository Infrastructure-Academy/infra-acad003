### The Amplitude–Drag Curve: 1840 → 2025

Between 1840 and 1978, China entered a prolonged high-Drag phase:

- **1840** — First Opium War. External interference fractures the network.
- **1900** — Boxer Rebellion. Systemic inertia compounds with foreign occupation.
- **1911** — End of Imperial Rule. Administrative continuity severed.
- **1949** — Civil war concludes. Internal reconnection begins, but systemic inertia persists.
- **1966–1976** — Cultural Revolution. Institutional memory attacked. Drag maximised.

Throughout this 140-year period, **Amplitude (A) was suppressed but never destroyed.** The civilisational substrate — Confucian bureaucratic tradition, family networks, shared written language, spatial infrastructure (canals, roads, urban grids) — remained intact beneath the surface.

The Third Plenary Session (December 1978) converted systemic inertia into thrust:
