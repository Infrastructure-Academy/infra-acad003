Interpretation &mdash; Why This Song, This Moment
Fish wrote Fugazi as a condemnation of societal indifference &mdash; a world sleepwalking through decay, &ldquo;drowning in the real,&rdquo; distracted by &ldquo;aural contraceptives aborting pregnant conversation.&rdquo; The song moves through London&rsquo;s broken infrastructure &mdash; Piccadilly line rat race, Brixton chess, Embankment&rsquo;s homeless &mdash; cataloguing a civilisation that has stopped building and started consuming itself.
The climax is not despair. It is a demand: &ldquo;Where are the prophets, where are the visionaries, where are the poets?&rdquo; Fish is not asking rhetorically. He is calling them into existence. And then he dedicates the song to them &mdash; to the poets, the prophets, the visionaries. He is saying: you are the answer to the question the song asks.
