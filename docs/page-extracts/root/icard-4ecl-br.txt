Significance for iAAi
The BR explicitly includes &ldquo;Publishing Services&rdquo; alongside Engineering and Trading. This means 4ECL is already legally authorised to conduct book publishing, ebook sales, print-on-demand distribution, and all digital product sales in Hong Kong without requiring any additional licensing or registration amendments.
Combined with &ldquo;Engineering&rdquo; and &ldquo;Trading,&rdquo; the registration covers the full scope of iAAi&rsquo;s commercial activities: consulting, platform operation, book sales, merchandise, IP licensing, and data intelligence services.
Hong Kong Tax Jurisdiction
Sales Tax / VAT
0%
HK charges zero sales tax, VAT, or GST on all goods and services including ebooks and digital products
Profits Tax (First HK$2M)
8.25%
Two-tier system &mdash; first HK$2M (~US$256K) of assessable profits at concessionary rate
Profits Tax (Above HK$2M)
16.5%
Standard rate for profits exceeding the HK$2M threshold
Withholding Tax
0%
No withholding tax on royalties, dividends, or payments to non-residents
Territorial Principle: HK taxes only profits sourced within Hong Kong. International online sales where the customer is overseas and the transaction is concluded via a website hosted outside HK may not be subject to HK profits tax. This is a significant structural advantage for a globally-distributed digital product.
