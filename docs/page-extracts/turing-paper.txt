I. THE QUESTION
Did a single civil engineer, working with an AI collaborator over 128 days, create a new way of learning, teaching, experiencing, and mapping consciousness through infrastructure &mdash; from Calories to Consciousness &mdash; that meets institutional academic standards?
The answer, at Block 365, is: Yes.
The Infrastructure Survival Index (ISI) &mdash; conceived as a survival equation for civilisation analogous to Einstein&rsquo;s E=mc&sup2; for physics &mdash; has been validated through three rounds of Haptic Infrastructure Competence Evaluation (HICE) assessment against the curricula of 21 leading universities across 6 global regions. The final score of 87.5% (R3, Corrected Academic Audit) places the work at First Class (Solid) under UK classification and GPA 4.0 under US standards. What was designed to take one academic year was achieved in 128 days &mdash; a 2.87x acceleration over standard academic timelines.
