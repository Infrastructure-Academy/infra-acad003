PROLOGUE: FIRE
The First Spark of Intent
Before the first tool, before the first word, there was Fire. Not merely the random lightning strike or the volcanic eruption, but Fire tamed, contained, and wielded by nascent humanity. This was the primordial spark of intent, the initial act of the Master Builder and the Master Weaver, a founding relay in the grand Civilisational Relay that would stretch from the primal forest to the farthest reaches of the cosmos.
Twelve thousand years ago, a great change erupted of unimaginable proportions. In an explosion of intent, man rose from hunter and prey to apex predator. The seed was sown, the threads spun, and the age of intelligence began. Civilisation ignited &mdash; an epic of incalculable possibility. United, mankind could build the impossible, raising cathedrals in the sky and unlocking the ethereal depths of our being.
Division brings collapse, but unification multiplies strength &mdash; the grand design that lifts us toward higher intelligence and higher learning. What can be imagined may now be achieved, for imagination itself has become infrastructure.
