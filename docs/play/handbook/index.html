iGO PLAYER'S HANDBOOK

Rules, progression, and mastery guide

WHAT IS iGO?

iGO is the Infrastructure Academy's gamified learning platform. Players spin the Relay Spinner to explore the 12 Civilisational Relays, collect iCARDS, and progress through mastery tiers.

The live game is hosted at isicalcexp-bqjsukv3.manus.space

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Spin the Relay Spinner — Land on a relay (R01–R12) to begin exploration
  2. Explore the Relay — Read the relay page, watch the video, or explore in DAVID Reality Engine
  3. Answer Challenge Questions — Test your understanding to earn iCARDS
  4. Collect iCARDS — Each relay has unique cards representing key concepts, inventions, and figures
  5. Advance Tiers — Collect cards to progress from Observer to Architect

MASTERY TIERS

TIER 1 Observer 0–9 iCARDS
TIER 2 Student 10–29 iCARDS
TIER 3 Scholar 30–49 iCARDS
TIER 4 Architect 50+ iCARDS

GAME MODES

  • 🎰 Relay Spinner — Random relay selection with challenge questions
  • 🏰 Dungeon Crawl — Progressive relay exploration with increasing difficulty
  • 🧠 Grey Matter — Deep-dive analytical challenges for advanced players
  • 🌍 DAVID Reality Engine — 3D spatial exploration of the full relay lattice
