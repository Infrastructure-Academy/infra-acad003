UN SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOALS

How the Infrastructure Academy framework maps to global targets

SDG 4 — QUALITY EDUCATION

Infrastructure Academy is built as an educational engine. The 12 Relays framework teaches systems thinking, lifecycle analysis, and interdisciplinary connection. The iGO game turns learning into play. The DAVID Reality Engine makes abstract concepts tangible. Every page, every card, every video is designed to educate across 8 languages.

SDG 9 — INDUSTRY, INNOVATION & INFRASTRUCTURE

The entire framework is a taxonomy of infrastructure. Every relay — from Fire to Human Nodes — is an infrastructure system. The ISI Calculator quantifies infrastructure resilience. The La Menara series explores how infrastructure shapes industrial and innovation capacity across all five webs.

SDG 11 — SUSTAINABLE CITIES & COMMUNITIES

The 4C Threats analysis (Conflict, Contagion, Climate, Cost) directly addresses urban resilience. The framework shows how cities have always been infrastructure systems — from river civilisations to modern megacities. The ISI Calculator measures city-scale infrastructure survival probability.

SDG 17 — PARTNERSHIPS FOR THE GOALS

Infrastructure Academy is designed for collaboration — academic, industry, governmental, and public. The open-source model, the Share Kit, the outreach templates, and the iGO game are all built to be shared, remixed, and deployed by partners worldwide. The 5 Great Webs framework itself models partnership: Physical, Biological, Digital, Social, and Consciousness infrastructure must all cooperate.
