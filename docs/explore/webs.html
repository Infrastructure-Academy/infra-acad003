THE 5 GREAT WEBS

Every civilisational relay operates across five interconnected webs — the lenses through which infrastructure shapes human experience

🔴 PHYSICAL
Materials & Engineering. Roads, buildings, machines, energy systems. The tangible substrate of civilisation. Every relay begins here — fire's temperature ladder, tree's timber, road's stone and asphalt.
🟢 BIOLOGICAL
Food, Medicine & Life. Agriculture, medicine, ecology, bioengineering. The web that sustains bodies. From tree's apple-to-cider chain to engine's thermodynamic limits on human endurance.
🔵 DIGITAL
Data, Code & Networks. Computing, telecommunications, information theory. The loom's punch cards, the telegraph following rails, 95% of internet via ship-borne cables, Starlink in orbit.
🟡 SOCIAL
Law, Trade & Governance. Markets, legal systems, institutions, diplomacy. The Royal Road's postal laws, maritime law from 800 BCE, railway standard time zones, internet governance.
🟣 CONSCIOUSNESS
Myth, Spirit & Identity. Religion, philosophy, culture, meaning-making. Prometheus and Agni, Yggdrasil and the Bodhi Tree, the Ship of Theseus, the Tao as road. Infrastructure becomes mythology.
