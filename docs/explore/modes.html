3 MODES OF CIVILISATION

How humanity has powered progress across 12,000 years

MODE 1 — MAGIC

Relays 1–4: Fire, Tree, River, Horse

The era of biological and elemental power. Human and animal muscle, controlled fire, natural materials. No external energy storage — everything powered by calories and combustion. The foundation layer of all subsequent civilisation.

MODE 2 — MUSCLE

Relays 5–9: Roads, Ships, Loom, Rail, Engine

The era of mechanical multiplication. Machines that amplified human capability 100x — the steam engine, the power loom, the railway. Energy became stored, transportable, and controllable at scale.

MODE 3 — MACHINE

Relays 10–12: AAA Triad, Orbit, Human Nodes

The era of autonomous and networked systems. Self-driving vehicles, satellite constellations, artificial intelligence. Machines that not only multiply human power but begin to make decisions independently. The torus closes: machine feeds back to programmer.
