THE 4 PILLARS

The analytical framework behind every relay in Volume 2

PILLAR 1 — OBSERVATIONAL

What exists? The raw data layer. Dimensions, materials, construction methods, operational parameters. Every relay is measured, weighed, and mapped. The observational pillar is the foundation — no analysis without accurate observation.

PILLAR 2 — EDUCATIONAL

What does it teach? The pedagogy layer. How each relay advances knowledge, skills, and systems thinking. The iGO game is built on this pillar — every spin, every card, every dungeon level encodes educational content across all 5 Webs.

PILLAR 3 — APPLICATION

Where is it used? The practical layer. Real-world implementations, case studies, engineering applications. The ISI Calculator operationalises this pillar — turning framework theory into measurable infrastructure resilience scores.

PILLAR 4 — THESIS

What does it mean? The meaning layer. Philosophical, historical, and civilisational significance. The 4Elements Consulting encoded thesis chain and the Trust vs. Belief Professions framework live here. The thesis pillar answers why any of this matters.
