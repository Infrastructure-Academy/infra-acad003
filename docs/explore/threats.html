THE 4C THREATS

The four forces that degrade infrastructure and civilisation

🔴 CONFLICT

War, terrorism, civil unrest, and resource competition. Infrastructure is the first casualty of conflict — bridges destroyed, power grids targeted, supply chains severed. The Infrastructure Survival Index (ISI) collapses when conflict exceeds institutional capacity.

🟢 CONTAGION

Pandemic, biosecurity, and disease vectors. COVID-19 revealed how quickly contagion can collapse global supply chains, empty cities, and freeze construction. Health infrastructure ISI is inseparable from economic infrastructure ISI.

🔵 CLIMATE

Extreme weather, sea-level rise, resource depletion, and ecological collapse. Every relay in the Infrastructure Academy framework is vulnerable to climate: roads flood, rails warp, engines overheat, orbit decays. Climate ISI is the defining constraint of the 21st century.

🟡 COST

Debt, inflation, resource scarcity, and maintenance backlog. Infrastructure decays exponentially when maintenance funding is deferred. The Cost Crisis is invisible until bridges collapse, pipes burst, and power fails. The ISI Calculator quantifies this decay in real time.
