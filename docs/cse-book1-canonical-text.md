# CSE Book 1 Foundations — Canonical Relay Definitions (Extracted from v10)

## Chapter 2 — The 12 Civilisational Relays

### Relay 1 — Fire (Energy Ignition)
- **Emergence:** 10,000 BCE
- **Function:** Transform matter → energy
- **Memory Function:** Converts potential → action; enables cooking → brain expansion; establishes first recursive loop (heat → survival → learning)
- **Modern Expression:** Combustion engines, industrial heat processes, power generation
- **Anchor Examples:** Control of Fire (1.5M BCE), Neolithic agriculture, Industrial furnaces

### Relay 2 — Tree (Material Cycle)
- **Emergence:** 8,000 BCE
- **Function:** Biological recursion
- **Memory Function:** Renewable material cycles; long-duration biological persistence; structural stability (wood → shelter → settlement)
- **Modern Expression:** Forestry systems, biomaterials, ecological infrastructure

### Relay 3 — River (Hydraulic Continuity)
- **Emergence:** 6,000–3,000 BCE
- **Function:** Natural persistence
- **Memory Function:** Continuous flow → continuous memory; hydraulic stability → agricultural surplus; predictable revisitation cycles
- **Modern Expression:** Water supply, wastewater, stormwater, irrigation

### Relay 4 — Horse (Mobility Vector)
- **Emergence:** 4,000 BCE
- **Function:** Repeated traversal
- **Memory Function:** Expands civilisational radius; enables trade, warfare, communication; creates first long-distance revisitation loops

### Relay 5 — Roads (Connectivity)
- **Emergence:** 500 BCE
- **Function:** Resistance reduction
- **Memory Function:** Lowers β (drag); standardises movement; creates predictable intervals
- **Modern Expression:** National road networks, urban transport grids

### Relay 6 — Ships (Exchange)
- **Emergence:** 500 CE
- **Function:** Cross-domain reinforcement
- **Memory Function:** Connects civilisations; transfers surplus; enables maritime recursion

### Relay 7 — Loom (Information Weaving)
- **Emergence:** 1780 CE
- **Function:** Fractal revisitation
- **Memory Function:** Pattern recognition; binary logic; algorithmic repetition
- **Modern Expression:** Telecom, data centres, digital identity

### Relay 8 — Rail (Acceleration)
- **Emergence:** 1830 CE
- **Function:** Interval compression
- **Memory Function:** High-frequency revisitation; spatial compression; temporal synchronisation

### Relay 9 — Engine (Power)
- **Emergence:** 1850 CE
- **Function:** Mechanical amplitude
- **Memory Function:** Mechanical repetition; industrial scaling; high-energy recursion

### Relay 10 — AAA Triad (Auto/Aviation/Airwaves)
- **Emergence:** 1900 CE
- **Function:** Multi-modal learning
- **Memory Function:** Multi-modal learning; urban expansion; real-time communication

### Relay 11 — Orbit (Planetary Systems)
- **Emergence:** 1960 CE
- **Function:** Long-interval persistence
- **Memory Function:** Long-interval sensing; global monitoring; climate intelligence

### Relay 12 — Human Nodes (Consciousness)
- **Emergence:** 2000 CE
- **Function:** Infinite recursion
- **Memory Function:** Generational transfer; cognitive saturation; civilisational self-awareness
- **Note:** Relay 12 is the apex. It activates all others.

## Chapter 5 — The Signal Formula and the 4Cs

### Signal Formula
```
S = (A × P) / β
```
- **A** — Amplitude (encoding strength)
- **P** — Persistence (revisitation frequency)
- **β** — Resistance (aggregate drag of the 4Cs)

### The 4Cs (Resistance Forces)
- **C1 — Conflict:** War, instability, fragmentation. Raises β sharply.
- **C2 — Contagion:** Biological or informational spread. Destabilises systems.
- **C3 — Climate:** Environmental shocks. Damages infrastructure.
- **C4 — Cost:** Economic pressure. Reduces maintenance and revisitation.

### 4Rs (Counter-Forces)
- **R1 — Revelation:** Knowledge, transparency, education
- **R2 — Resilience:** Adaptation, redundancy, recovery
- **R3 — Regeneration:** Renewal, circular economy, restoration
- **R4 — Recursion:** Self-reinforcement, feedback loops, continuous improvement

## Chapter 4 — Forgetting Curve & Remembrance Curve

### Forgetting Curve (Natural Decay)
```
R(t) = R₀ × e^(-t/S₀)
```

### Remembrance Curve (Engineered)
```
R(t) = 1 - e^(-n/N)
```
- n = number of revisits
- N = spacing scale (target horizon)

### Canonical Remembrance Equation
```
R(t) = R₀e^(-t/S₀) + k × ((A·P)/β) × (1 - e^(-n/N))
```

## ISI Equation
```
ISI = min(1.0, (A·P/β) × (1 - e^(-n/N)))
```
