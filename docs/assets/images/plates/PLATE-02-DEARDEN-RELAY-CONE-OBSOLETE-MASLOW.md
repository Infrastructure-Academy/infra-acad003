## CSE Mapping

### The Maslow Precedent
Maslow (1943) proposed a 5-tier pyramid of human motivation:
1. Physiological
2. Safety
3. Belonging/Love
4. Esteem
5. Self-Actualisation

### The Dearden Inversion
CSE treats Maslow's pyramid as a *symptom* of infrastructure, not a cause. Each Maslow tier is an emergent property of a specific relay operating at scale:
- Physiological = R01–R03 (Fire, Tree, River) — caloric and water security
- Safety = R04–R06 (Horse, Road, Ship) — mobility and territorial control
- Belonging = R07–R08 (Loom, Rail) — trade networks create cultural exchange
- Esteem = R09–R10 (Engine, AAA Triad) — industrial capacity as national status
- Self-Actualisation = R11–R12 (Orbit, Human Nodes) — species-scale consciousness
