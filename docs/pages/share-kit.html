Week 2: The Loom Changed Everything

1780. The Jacquard loom used punched cards to automate weaving. Those punched cards became the ancestor of: - Player pianos - Census tabulators (Hollerith, 1890) - Early computers (Colossus, ENIAC) - Every line of code ever written The loom was the first programmable machine. It industrialised not just fabric, but logic itself. Relay 7 of 12. The Infrastructure Odyssey: infrastructure-academy.com What's the most surprising infrastructure origin story you know?

Week 3: Roads = Empire

500 BCE. The Persian Royal Road: 2,700 km from Susa to Sardis. A messenger could travel it in 9 days. Without it, the empire couldn't govern. Rome copied. Then improved. Then standardised. 80,000 km of Roman roads still shape Europe today. Infrastructure isn't a consequence of empire. Infrastructure IS empire.
