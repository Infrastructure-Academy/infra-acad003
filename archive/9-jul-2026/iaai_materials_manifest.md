## Preamble

This manifest constitutes the formal inventory of the iAAi materials archive as received and catalogued to 9 July 2026. The archive represents a partial transfer of a total collection of **3,796 items**; approximately **2,400 images remain untransferred** and are recorded in Section 9 (Known Missing). A critical archival note governs the reading of this manifest: **materials arrived most-recent-first**, meaning that earlier batches represent current canon while later batches document historical evolution. Within the photographic record, higher IMG numbers correspond to more recent captures under iOS sequential numbering (subject to counter rollover, noted in Section 7).

The archive is organised into eight received categories and one deficit register. Each category is catalogued below with structural notes relevant to its position within the CSE framework.
