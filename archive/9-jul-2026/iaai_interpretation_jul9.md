> **Warp** is structure — the long threads held under tension: the 12 relays, the 5 Webs, the 5 Pillars, the lattice coordinates of the Dearden Field.

This is what the phrase **"interlaced context"** denotes. The archive is not a pile of files to be summarised; it is a weft to be passed through the warp of the framework, over and under, until the pattern appears.
