## Architecture Interpretation

These seven plates form a complete visual statement of the iCU governance model:

- **Plate 1-2** (Relay Cone): Civilisational scale infrastructure, top-down. R12 is where humans enter.
- **Plate 3** (System Node): The conversion mechanism — unengaged signal is zero; engaged participant becomes one.
- **Plate 4** (Engagement Point): The act of engagement creates the bridge.
- **Plate 5** (FC13/iCU Mirror): The interface itself — what the human sees and the civilisation records.
- **Plate 6-7** (Full synthesis): Maslow (individual, 1943) rendered obsolete by Dearden (civilisational, 2026), meeting at iCU = 0.

## Key Canonical Text Captured

> "Zero means nothing compared to 1 that means everything — yet zero makes the system work."

This replaces the prior "system node = 0" assumption. The correct reading:
- **0 = potential / unrecorded signal** (the unengaged human at civilisational scale)
- **1 = participant / recorded signal** (the docked iCU with ICE-HQ active)
- **0 makes the system work** because without the pre-docking pool of unrecorded signals, there is no reservoir of potential participants. The system's health is measured by its capacity to convert 0→1, not by the number of 1s already active.
