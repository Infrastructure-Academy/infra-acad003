## What was integrated

The new `iCU Canon` worksheet records the complete interpretation of the iCU intent developed from the supplied plate, the framework paper, and the test-agent feedback. It defines iCU as four related operational layers: **Infrastructure Connection Unit** as the technical 0/1 docking gate; **Individual Cognition Unit** as the identifiable human capacity node; **Intensive Care Unit** as the stabilisation condition for human–machine governance; and **I See You / Eye See You** as mutual recognition with the human gaze retaining sovereignty over the machine instrument.

The tab also records **ICE as the whole human operator**, the integrated equation `H = I ⊗ C ⊗ E = Human Quotient (HQ)`, and **CQ as the created and built output** of the civil engineer/operator. It adds the **Driver Paradigm**, which states that safe adoption can precede complete technical understanding, and converts the iCU meaning into morning-dock, continuous-monitor, and evening-undock field checks with pass, failure, and recovery states.

The scale section records the **Maslow–Dearden mirror**: individual human development and the Dearden Relay Cone operate at different scales but share a survival foundation, meeting at the iCU/FC13 interface. The dominion boundary states that technical connection is never confused with authority, and that an identifiable human remains responsible for intention, action, and review.
