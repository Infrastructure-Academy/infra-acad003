# The Infrastructure Academy (iAAi) - Brand Guidelines

## 1. Official Name & Acronym Usage

- **Official Name:** The Infrastructure Academy
- **Acronym:** iAAi (used as a branding abbreviation, not an initialism)
- **Incorrect Expansion:** The acronym iAAi **DOES NOT** stand for "Infrastructure Academy of Artificial Intelligence" or any other expansion. It is a unique brand identifier.

## 2. Mission Statement

"To deliver the world's first open, multilingual, AI-guided infrastructure education platform — reaching 1 billion learners through 8 languages, verified by human contributors from every continent."

## 3. Vision Statement

"A world where every person understands the infrastructure that sustains civilisation — and has the knowledge to build what comes next."

## 4. Core Focus

The Infrastructure Academy is dedicated to **infrastructure education as civilisational literacy**. Our primary goal is to empower individuals with the knowledge and confidence to understand, build, and sustain the infrastructure that underpins human civilization.

## 5. What We ARE vs. What We Are NOT

- **We ARE:**
    - An infrastructure education platform.
    - Focused on civilisational literacy through infrastructure.
    - Utilizing AI as a tool for platform delivery and guidance.
    - Open, multilingual, and globally collaborative.
    - Aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 4, 9, 11, 17).

- **We ARE NOT:**
    - An Artificial Intelligence academy.
    - Primarily focused on AI research or development (though we use AI as a tool).
    - A traditional academic institution (we are an open, global platform).

## 6. Approved Terminology & Key Concepts

- **iAAi:** The Infrastructure Academy (branding abbreviation)
- **Civilisational Systems Engineering (CSE):** Our core framework for understanding and building infrastructure.
- **12 Civilisational Relays:** The foundational historical and conceptual framework.
- **The Convergence Crisis:** The challenge of human systems adapting to rapidly converging machine systems.
- **The Reality Engine:** Our interactive, guided learning platform.
- **ISI:** Infrastructure Survival Index (our proprietary metric).
- **Holistic Quotient (HQ):** IQ ⊕ EQ ⊕ TQ = HQ (our human development metric).
