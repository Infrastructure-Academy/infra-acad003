### 5. The Signal Formula

Every civilisational relay transmits a signal. The strength of that signal determines whether the relay persists or decays. The formula that governs signal strength is:

> **S = (A × P) / β**

| Symbol | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| A | Amplitude | Reach — how far the signal travels |
| P | Persistence | Duration — how long the signal lasts |
| β | Resistance | The 4Cs — the forces that attenuate the signal |

When β approaches zero, the signal propagates indefinitely. When β exceeds the product of A and P, the relay dies. The Academy exists to maximise A and P while reducing β — extending the survival window of civilisational knowledge.

The Survival Formula variant: S = (A × P) / β where β = 0.000267% — the threshold below which civilisational memory persists across generations.
