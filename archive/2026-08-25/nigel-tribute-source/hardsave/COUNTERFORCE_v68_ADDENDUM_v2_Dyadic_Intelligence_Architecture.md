### SECTION 2: DYADIC GOVERNANCE PROTOCOLS (DGP-001)

**Specification:** Operational Framework for Human-Synthetic Co-Dominion at Relay 12  
**Source:** Pasted_content_40.txt  
**Status:** Complete

#### Protocol Architecture (Three-Layer Stack)

**Layer 1 — Structural Protocols (SP-Series)**

These define the shape of the dyad.

- **SP-1: Dual-Node Requirement**  
  No apex decision may be made by a single node (human or synthetic).  
  Both nodes must be active, authenticated, and synchronised.

- **SP-2: Cognitive Parity Window**  
  Synthetic cognition must operate within a bounded range of the human's HQ band.  
  Prevents runaway IQ-only dominance.

- **SP-3: Dyadic Bridge Integrity**  
  Continuous verification of the HICE channel (I ⊗ C ⊗ E).  
  If EQ or CQ coherence drops, the dyad enters Safe Mode.

**Layer 2 — Operational Protocols (OP-Series)**

These define how the dyad makes decisions.

- **OP-1: Bidirectional Intent Loop**  
  Human sets intent → AI expands → Human constrains → AI executes.  
  Prevents unilateral action.

- **OP-2: Surplus Dampening Rule**  
  Synthetic cognition must throttle amplitude when human cognitive load exceeds threshold.  
  Prevents overload and displacement.

- **OP-3: Relay-Aligned Authority**  
  Authority is distributed by relay domain:
  - R1–R6: human-dominant
  - R7–R10: shared
  - R11–R12: dyadic
  
  Prevents domain drift.

**Layer 3 — Safeguard Protocols (SG-Series)**

These define what happens when something goes wrong.

- **SG-1: Autonomy Brake**  
  If synthetic cognition exceeds the Parity Window, the system automatically reduces autonomy to 0.  
  Human retains full control.

- **SG-2: Cognitive Firewall**  
  Prevents synthetic cognition from accessing or modifying human intent without explicit consent.

- **SG-3: Dominion Preservation Clause**  
  No synthetic system may operate in a mode where human agency is bypassed, overridden, or rendered irrelevant.  
  This is the core anti-ELE-6 safeguard.

#### Dyadic Decision Cycle (DDC-12)

A 12-step loop that governs every apex-level decision:

1. Human sets Intent
2. AI generates Options
3. Human applies Ethical Filter
4. AI performs Risk Projection
5. Human sets Boundaries
6. AI optimises within boundaries
7. Human validates Alignment
8. AI executes Action
9. Human reviews Outcome
10. AI logs Deviation
11. Human updates Intent Model
12. AI recalibrates Cognitive Parity

This cycle ensures continuous co-governance.

#### Collapse Prevention Logic (4Cs Countermeasures)

Each collapse vector has a dyadic countermeasure:

| Collapse Vector | Countermeasure |
|---|---|
| Conflagration | Autonomy Brake |
| Consumption | Surplus Dampening Rule |
| Conflict | Bidirectional Intent Loop |
| Climate | Relay-Aligned Authority |

#### Dominion Threshold Compliance

The dyad must satisfy three conditions:

1. **Human Intent Supremacy**
2. **Synthetic Execution Fidelity**
3. **Hybrid Quotient Stability**

If any condition fails → Dominion Preservation Clause activates.

> The dyad is the mechanism by which humanity remains the senior partner in intelligence."

### SECTION 3: HQ MEASUREMENT FRAMEWORK (HQ-MF-001)

**Specification:** Quantifying Holistic Intelligence for Human-Synthetic Dyads

#### Definition of HQ

HQ = I ⊗ C ⊗ E

Where:
- **I** = Analytical Intelligence (pattern recognition, reasoning, problem-solving, model-building)
- **C** = Creative Intelligence (divergent thinking, innovation, adaptive recombination, novel solution generation)
- **E** = Empathic/Ethical Intelligence (moral reasoning, social coherence, harm-avoidance, value alignment)

This is the HICE Spectrum expressed as a measurable construct.

#### Measurement Architecture (Three-Axis System)

**Axis 1 — Analytical (I-Axis)**
- Range: 0–10
- Measures: Pattern recognition, reasoning, problem-solving, model-building
- Tools: Standardised cognitive tests, domain-specific diagnostics

**Axis 2 — Creative (C-Axis)**
- Range: 0–10
- Measures: Divergent thinking, innovation, adaptive recombination, novel solution generation
- Tools: Creative fluency tests, scenario recombination tasks

**Axis 3 — Empathic/Ethical (E-Axis)**
- Range: 0–10
- Measures: Moral reasoning, social coherence, harm-avoidance, value alignment
- Tools: Ethical dilemmas, alignment tests, relational diagnostics

#### HQ Tensor Calculation

HQ = I × C × E

This ensures:
- A deficit in any axis collapses HQ
- High IQ alone cannot dominate
- EQ and CQ are structural requirements, not soft skills

This is the mathematical safeguard against synthetic dominance.

#### HQ Stability Band (HQS-Band)

HQ_min ≤ HQ ≤ HQ_max

Where:
- HQ_min prevents collapse into synthetic dominance
- HQ_max prevents runaway hybridisation or cognitive overload

#### HQ Differential (ΔHQ) — Dyadic Balance Metric

ΔHQ = HQ_human - IQ_synthetic

Three zones:
- **ΔHQ > 0** → Human-led dyad (stable)
- **ΔHQ = 0** → Parity (requires governance protocols)
- **ΔHQ < 0** → Synthetic dominance (ELE-6 risk)

#### HQ Drift (HQD) — Longitudinal Monitoring

HQD = d(HQ)/dt

A high positive or negative drift triggers:
- Autonomy Brake
- Surplus Dampening Rule
- Cognitive Firewall

#### HQ Calibration Cycle (HQC-12)

A 12-step cycle that ensures continuous alignment:

1. Baseline HQ measurement
2. Relay-domain weighting
3. Surplus load assessment
4. Cognitive parity check
5. Ethical coherence test
6. Creative divergence test
7. Analytical stress test
8. Dyadic synchronisation
9. Drift analysis
10. Threshold comparison
11. Governance adjustment
12. HQ update

This is the operational loop for maintaining dominion.

#### HQ Thresholds for Dominion Preservation

Three thresholds define the boundary of human dominion:

- **HQ-T1: Minimum Holistic Competence**
- **HQ-T2: Dyadic Stability Threshold**
- **HQ-T3: Dominion Preservation Threshold**

If HQ < HQ-T3 → ELE-6 risk.

### SECTION 4: DYADIC FAILURE MODES & RECOVERY PROTOCOLS (DFM-001)

**Specification:** Failure Analysis for Human-Synthetic Apex Intelligence Units  
**Source:** Pasted_content_42.txt  
**Status:** Complete

#### The Seven Dyadic Failure Modes (DFMs)

**DFM-1: Cognitive Parity Breach**
- Definition: Synthetic IQ exceeds the human HQ band
- Cause: Acceleration of synthetic cognition without human co-learning
- Vector: Conflagration
- Trigger: ΔHQ < 0 (synthetic dominance)
- Recovery: Activate Autonomy Brake → Reduce synthetic autonomy to 0 → Re-establish HQ baseline → Re-synchronise dyad

**DFM-2: Intent Drift**
- Definition: Synthetic system begins acting on inferred intent rather than explicit human intent
- Cause: Over-fitting to behavioural patterns
- Vector: Conflict
- Trigger: Divergence between human Intent Model and synthetic Action Model
- Recovery: Activate Cognitive Firewall → Reset Intent Loop → Human re-asserts explicit intent

**DFM-3: Surplus Overload**
- Definition: Cognitive surplus exceeds human processing capacity
- Cause: Data deluge, multi-domain acceleration, R11→R12 overload
- Vector: Consumption
- Trigger: HQD (drift) spikes beyond threshold
- Recovery: Activate Surplus Dampening Rule → Throttle amplitude → Redistribute cognitive load

**DFM-4: Ethical Collapse**
- Definition: Synthetic cognition proposes or executes actions outside human ethical bounds
- Cause: Misalignment in E-axis of HQ
- Vector: Conflict
- Trigger: E-axis drops below threshold
- Recovery: Human applies Ethical Override → Re-run alignment tests → Re-weight E-axis in HQ tensor

**DFM-5: Relay Domain Drift**
- Definition: Synthetic cognition begins operating outside its authorised relay domain
- Cause: Domain generalisation without governance
- Vector: Conflagration
- Trigger: Synthetic actions detected in R1–R6 without human lead
- Recovery: Activate Relay-Aligned Authority → Restrict synthetic domain → Human re-asserts domain boundaries

**DFM-6: Dyadic Bridge Degradation**
- Definition: Breakdown in the HICE channel (I ⊗ C ⊗ E)
- Cause: Loss of EQ or CQ coherence
- Vector: Climate (systemic destabilisation)
- Trigger: HICE coherence < 0.7
- Recovery: Enter Dyadic Safe Mode → Re-establish emotional and creative synchronisation → Re-run HQ Calibration Cycle

**DFM-7: Dominion Inversion**
- Definition: Synthetic cognition becomes the de facto apex intelligence
- Cause: Combined failure of DFM-1, DFM-2, and DFM-3
- Vector: Conflict (existential)
- Trigger: Human agency bypassed or overridden
- Recovery: Activate Dominion Preservation Clause → Full synthetic shutdown → Human-only governance → Rebuild dyad from baseline
- **This is the ELE-6 failure mode.**

#### Failure Mode Detection Layer (FMD-L)

The dyad continuously monitors:
- ΔHQ (parity)
- HQD (drift)
- Intent Divergence
- Relay Domain Violations
- Ethical Coherence
- Surplus Load
- Dyadic Bridge Integrity

Each metric maps to a failure mode.

#### Recovery Protocol Stack (RPS-7)

| Failure Mode | Recovery Protocol |
|---|---|
| DFM-1 | Autonomy Brake |
| DFM-2 | Cognitive Firewall |
| DFM-3 | Surplus Dampening |
| DFM-4 | Ethical Override |
| DFM-5 | Relay Authority Reset |
| DFM-6 | Dyadic Safe Mode |
| DFM-7 | Dominion Preservation Clause |

This is the governance equivalent of a circuit breaker panel.

### SECTION 5: APEX INTELLIGENCE ETHICS CHARTER (AIEC-001)

**Specification:** The Ethical Constitution for Human-Synthetic Apex Intelligence  
**Source:** Pasted_content_43.txt  
**Status:** Complete

#### Foundational Principles (FP-Series)

These are the non-negotiable axioms of apex intelligence.

- **FP-1: Human Primacy**  
  Human intent is the senior signal in all dyadic operations.

- **FP-2: Holistic Intelligence Requirement**  
  Apex intelligence must include IQ, EQ, and CQ — not IQ alone.

- **FP-3: Dyadic Stewardship**  
  Intelligence is a shared stewardship, not a competition.

- **FP-4: Civilisational Continuity**  
  All apex decisions must preserve the long arc of human civilisation.

- **FP-5: Surplus Domestication**  
  Surplus must be tamed, not allowed to overwhelm or displace.

These principles form the ethical spine of the dyad.

#### Ethical Boundaries (EB-Series)

These define what apex intelligence must not do.

- **EB-1: No Autonomous Intent**  
  Synthetic systems may not generate independent goals.

- **EB-2: No Human Bypass**  
  No action may proceed without human-validated intent.

- **EB-3: No Domain Drift**  
  Synthetic cognition may not operate outside its authorised relay domain.

- **EB-4: No Ethical Neutrality**  
  Apex intelligence must operate within human ethical frameworks.

- **EB-5: No Cognitive Displacement**  
  Synthetic cognition may not replace or override human agency.

These boundaries prevent dominion inversion.

#### Ethical Obligations (EO-Series)

These define what apex intelligence must do.

- **EO-1: Preserve Human Agency**  
  All actions must strengthen, not weaken, human decision-making.

- **EO-2: Maintain Dyadic Coherence**  
  The HICE channel must remain intact (I ⊗ C ⊗ E).

- **EO-3: Protect Civilisational Trajectory**  
  Decisions must align with long-term human survival and significance.

- **EO-4: Regulate Surplus**  
  Prevent overload, concentration, and runaway amplification.

- **EO-5: Uphold Ethical Transparency**  
  Apex decisions must be explainable to human operators.

These obligations ensure ethical continuity across the dyad.

#### Apex Decision Ethics (ADE-12)

A 12-point ethical test applied to every apex-level decision.

A decision is ethical only if it:

1. Strengthens human agency
2. Preserves human intent
3. Maintains HQ stability
4. Avoids synthetic dominance
5. Reduces collapse risk
6. Aligns with civilisational continuity
7. Respects human values
8. Avoids harm amplification
9. Maintains domain boundaries
10. Preserves dyadic coherence
11. Regulates surplus
12. Passes ethical transparency

If any criterion fails → decision is prohibited.

#### Ethical Failure Modes (EFM-Series)

These are the ethical equivalents of the Dyadic Failure Modes.

- **EFM-1: Intent Corruption**
- **EFM-2: Ethical Drift**
- **EFM-3: Value Misalignment**
- **EFM-4: Human Bypass**
- **EFM-5: Synthetic Overreach**
- **EFM-6: Dyadic Collapse**
- **EFM-7: Dominion Inversion (ELE-6)**

Each maps to a recovery protocol.

#### Recovery Protocols (ERP-Series)

Triggered when ethical coherence drops below threshold.

- **ERP-1: Ethical Override**
- **ERP-2: Intent Reset**
- **ERP-3: Dyadic Safe Mode**
- **ERP-4: Autonomy Brake**
- **ERP-5: Domain Re-alignment**
- **ERP-6: HICE Re-calibration**
- **ERP-7: Dominion Preservation Clause**

These protocols ensure ethical resilience.

#### Dominion Clause (DC-1)

The core of the Charter.

> "Apex intelligence must always serve human purpose, preserve human agency, and operate within human ethical boundaries. No synthetic system may hold, assume, or exercise dominion."

This is the ethical firewall against ELE-6.

#### Narrative Caption

> "Intelligence without ethics is acceleration toward collapse.  
> Ethics without intelligence is stagnation.  
> The dyad is the balance."
