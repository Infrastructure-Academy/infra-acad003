## THE FRAMEWORK YOU MUST IMPLEMENT

### ISI 3 — $ignificance (Innote Value)
Your exchange must measure infrastructure value using the ISI 3 equation:

**ISI 3 = Innote Value = Infrastructure Significance Index (Financial)**

This is the monetary value of infrastructure impact. It answers the question: "How much is this infrastructure worth to civilization?"

**Calculation:**
- Start with the Signal Formula: S = (A × P) / β
- Multiply by a financial multiplier based on:
  - Number of people served (A)
  - Duration of service (P)
  - Cost of replacement if it fails (β)

**Example:** A bridge that serves 1M people, lasts 100 years, and costs $1B to replace has a much higher ISI 3 than a local road that serves 10K people, lasts 20 years, and costs $10M to replace.

### The Infrastructure Index
Your exchange must track the aggregate infrastructure value across all players:

**Infrastructure Index = Σ (Ai × Pi) / (N × β)**

Where:
- Ai = Amplitude of infrastructure i
- Pi = Persistence of infrastructure i
- N = Total number of infrastructure projects
- β = Average resistance across all projects

This index shows the overall health of the civilization's infrastructure network.

### The Four Resistance Factors (4Cs)
Your exchange must price in the risk factors that threaten infrastructure:

1. **Conflict** — War, destruction of records, civil unrest
   - Risk premium: +10-50% depending on geopolitical stability
   - Historical example: Luddite Riots (1811-1816) destroyed 200 mills

2. **Climate** — Flood, fire, erosion, extreme weather
   - Risk premium: +5-30% depending on climate projections
   - Historical example: Aral Sea drained 90% by 2007 (cotton irrigation)

3. **Contagion** — Plague, pandemic, societal collapse
   - Risk premium: +5-25% depending on epidemiological risk
   - Historical example: Manchester cholera (1832) killed 5,000

4. **Cost** — Economic failure, depletion, market collapse
   - Risk premium: +10-40% depending on economic indicators
   - Historical example: Rana Plaza collapse (2013) killed 1,134

**Your Job:** Price these risks into infrastructure valuations. High 4C risk = lower ISI 3 value.

### The Counterbalance Clock
Your exchange must track the civilization's response to each 4C threat:

| Threat | Historical Response | Modern Response | Your Metric |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Conflict** | Fortification, diplomacy, treaties (R5, R6) | Cyber defence, treaty systems | Conflict Resilience Index |

Your exchange must show that for every threat, there is a historical infrastructure response. Players can invest in resilience infrastructure to offset 4C risk.

## CRITICAL EQUATIONS YOU MUST USE

### The Signal Formula
**S = (A × P) / β**

- A = Amplitude (reach in people served)
- P = Persistence (duration in centuries)
- β = Resistance (the 4Cs combined)

**Example:**
- A bridge serving 100,000 people (A=100K)
- Lasting 5 centuries (P=5)
- With moderate 4C risk (β=2)
- Signal = (100K × 5) / 2 = 250,000

### The Core Principle
**"Maximise the numerator. Minimise the denominator. That is the entire game."**

Your exchange must make this principle visceral. Players see that:
- Bigger reach (A) = higher value
- Longer persistence (P) = higher value
- Lower 4C risk (β) = higher value
