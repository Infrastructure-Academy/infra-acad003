## SECTION 9: ENGINEERING INTERPRETATION

### System Fragility Principle

**Primitive systems (A ≈ 1):**
- Tolerate high drag (D_max = 4.0)
- Robust but slow
- Can absorb significant resistance

**Advanced systems (A ≈ 9):**
- Tolerate moderate drag (D_max = 1.33)
- Powerful but fragile
- Require precise equilibrium

**Dyad systems (A ≥ 16):**
- Tolerate minimal drag (D_max ≤ 1.0)
- Maximum capability, maximum sensitivity
- Demand continuous testing and human oversight

### Operational Implication

**High capability = High fragility**

As systems advance, their tolerance for resistance decreases. The Seesaw therefore quantifies the dynamic equilibrium between capability and resistance, ensuring that AI acceleration does not bypass human testing.
