## THE TEACHING (What This Shows)

### Reading Age Proof
The 12 Relay names — Fire, Tree, River, Horse, Roads, Ships, Rail, Loom, Engine, AAA Triad, Orbit, Human Nodes — achieve an average Flesch-Kincaid grade level of 2 (age 7-8). Ten of twelve are single-syllable or two-syllable words that an 8-year-old can read, understand, and remember. This is not simplification — it is clarity. The complex world of 12,000 years of civilisational infrastructure reduced to 12 words a child can hold in their hand.

### The Tolkien Packaging Model
Tea with Tolkien proved the method: The Silmarillion (Tolkien's most complex work) made accessible through three packaging layers:
1. **Silmarillion Reader's Guide** — 43-page ebook (scholar level)
2. **Pocket Guide to Middle-earth** — printable booklet (apprentice level)
3. **30 Days in the Shire** — gamified journey (game level)

iAAi mirrors this exactly with the 4-tier model:
1. **Observational** — see the infrastructure (game mode: freeform)
2. **Educational** — learn the principles (game mode: semi-supervised with DAVID AI DM)
3. **Application** — apply to challenges (game mode: supervised with arbiter)
4. **Thesis** — develop philosophy (Turing Papers, TECTON lexicon)

### The Harnessed Power Sequence
Each stage of human history is a moment where man domesticated a new force:

| Stage | Force | HICE Score | What Man Gained |
|-------|-------|------------|-----------------|
| 1. Man | Fire, muscle | 4/4 | Warmth, cooking, tool-making |
| 2. Animal | Horse, ox | 3.5/4 | Speed, strength, companionship |
| 3. Machine | Loom, waterwheel | 0/4 | Repetition without fatigue |
| 4. Engine | Steam, combustion | 0/4 | Power without muscle |
| 5. Robosynthetics | AI, neural networks | 1/4 (I only) | Computation without presence |

**The key insight:** Animals achieved symbiosis with man because both have HICE — both are biological, both feel, both sense. AI has only I (Intellectual). It lacks H (Holistic awareness), C (Creative empathy), and E (Embodied sensation). Therefore the man-AI union cannot follow the domestication model. It requires a governance interface language — a contract — not biological bonding.

### What Worked Before Across All Webs
The 5 Webs (Physical, Biological, Digital, Social, Consciousness) × 12 Relays = 60 intersection points. Each relay domesticated a force that propagated across all 5 webs. The pattern is recursive and self-similar across millennia. Civil engineering has been running this loop since the first irrigation channel:

**Tender → Mobilise → Execute → Complete → Demobilise → Lessons Learned → Feed Next Tender**

This is not a metaphor. This is the operating system of human civilisation. The memorial website is the living proof.
