THE PROBLEM

WHY CHILDREN SAY NO

"Civil engineering" scares people. The phrase conjures hard hats, mud, and mathematics. But what engineers actually do is build. Building is what every child does with LEGO, jigsaws, pictures, and games. It is the natural state. We exist in it. We filter out the noise. We find solutions to problems. That is the mode — morally neutral, universally understood.

The problem is not that children dislike building. The problem is that nobody tells them the world they live in — every road, every bridge, every pipe, every wire — was built by someone who started exactly where they are. 85% of children reject engineering as a career because they have never been shown what it actually is.

55% of parents cannot confidently advise their children about engineering careers. 70% of young people say school never connected what they learned to the built environment around them. The pipeline is broken at the source.

Sources: EngineeringUK (2025), ASQ/Harris Interactive, IET Skills Survey
