THE CHAIN — 2.4 MILLION YEARS

10 Species of Homo

The genus Homo has produced at least 9 recognised species over 2.4 million years. Each represents a step in the infrastructure of consciousness — from the first stone tool to the first AI-augmented thesis. The 10th species is not biological evolution but infrastructural evolution — the moment when the builder's tools become extensions of consciousness itself.

1

Homo habilis

The Handyman

2.4 Mya

First stone tools — the Oldowan toolkit. Brain ~600cc. The moment infrastructure began.

2

Homo rudolfensis

The Debated One

1.9 Mya

Larger brain than habilis (~750cc). Contested classification — the first taxonomic uncertainty in the chain.

3

Homo erectus

The Upright

1.8 Mya

Mastered fire. First to leave Africa. Acheulean hand axes. Survived 1.5M years — the longest-reigning Homo species.

4

Homo heidelbergensis

The Hunter

700 Kya

Wooden spears, cooperative hunting. The common ancestor of both Neanderthals and Sapiens.

5

Homo neanderthalensis

The Thinker

400 Kya

Burial rituals, cave art, brain larger than Sapiens (~1,600cc). Built shelters. The first conscious builders.

6

Homo naledi

The Enigma

335–236 Kya*

Small brain but deliberate burial. Fossils date 335–236 Kya; species origin debated. Rising Star Cave, South Africa.

7

Homo sapiens

The Wise

300 Kya

Language, agriculture, cities, civilisation. Jebel Irhoud, Morocco. YOU ARE HERE.

8

Homo floresiensis

The Hobbit

100–50 Kya

1 metre tall, island-dwarfed. Flores, Indonesia. Hunted stegodon. Proof that evolution branches, not just climbs.

9

Homo luzonensis

The Islander

67 Kya

Callao Cave, Philippines. Curved finger bones suggest tree-climbing. Discovered 2019 — the chain is still growing.

10

Homo infrastructus

The Builder

2026 CE

The augmented species. AI-human symbiosis. Infrastructure consciousness made manifest. N + T = D.

* Homo naledi fossil dates 335–236 Kya; species origin debated. Sources: Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History, Nature, Science.
