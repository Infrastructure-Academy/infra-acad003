## Changes

1. **Figure A** retouched, not replaced. The light navigation plate is the same design, with the third working step now labelled Stop, Go, and Ratio (SGAR) rather than “Stop, Go, and the Ratio.” Caption A is unchanged.

2. **Preface** opening paragraph (only). Figure A is named as a control panel for the argument, as well as the paper mapped. Stop, Go, and Ratio (SGAR) is the operable stance, without the extra “the.” One closing sentence records that the paper is also a demonstration of the method: a protection and trust layer, engaged recursively at variable load and speed, safely. Paragraphs after this one, including the *Homo sapiens* zoom, are unchanged.

3. **Abstract.** A short definitional bridge now names machine intelligence as the working term for the AI–AGI–ASI continuum, of which Good’s (1966) first ultraintelligent machine is the far case, with his governance proviso. The full Good quotation remains in Section 2. The add was matched by a trim of a protocols clause that already appears in the preface, and by a tighter opening sentence. The abstract is 259 words (Draft 39 was 262). The UIM is not dated to 2050, and no runaway is stated as the outcome if the solution is late.

4. **Figure 5** added in Section 6, immediately after the Discovery → Harnessing → Training → Governance chain: the recut From Discovery to Preservation plate, as a companion to Figure A, not a replacement for it. Section 5 is the warrant; the four stages sit in Section 6; endurance emerges through Sections 7–11; Section 12 is held off the plate. The later body figures have been renumbered so first-citation order remains consecutive: the OODA/IUMC plate is now Figure 6, and the supervisory-sequence plate is now Figure 7. Figure 4a is unchanged.


6. **References typography** unified. Forty-five entries had been left in Helvetica 9 pt with single spacing, against fifteen entries (and the rest of the paper) in the document default, Arial 11 pt at 1.15 line spacing. All 60 entries now inherit that default. No wording was changed. Official Draft 38 and circulating Draft 39 were not overwritten.


7. **Abstract, public-concern note completed.** The opening sentence now names the full continuum: AI, AGI, ASI and Artificial Ultra-Intelligence (AUI — I.J. Good’s ultraintelligent machine). Machine intelligence remains the working term, with AUI as the far case and Good’s (1966) governance proviso. AUI is the paper’s working name for Good’s term, not an acronym he coined. Official Draft 38 and circulating Draft 39 were not overwritten.
